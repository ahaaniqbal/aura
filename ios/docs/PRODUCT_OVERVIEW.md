# Aura — product overview

*The assistant that remembers your people, and follows through.*

## What Aura is

Aura is an iPhone app that works like a sharp, caring friend with a perfect memory. You talk to it in plain words, English, Hindi or Hinglish: "met Priya at the gym, she loves tulips", "I need to call Sam today", "sam's birthday is june 28th". Aura quietly files the people, the promises, the dates and the tasks, and then does the one thing most apps never do: **it follows through.**

Aura is not a to-do list you maintain. It is a memory that maintains you.

## What makes it different

1. **It remembers people, not just tasks.** Birthdays with gift ideas attached. Promises, in both directions: what you owe and what's owed to you. Friends who have quietly gone silent for three weeks.
2. **Every task has an end.** Tell Aura what you need to do, and if you don't give a time, it asks: "till when do you want this done?" Answer in your own words: "6pm", "in 10 hours", "by evening". From that moment Aura paces **four nudges** across the time you gave it, at 25%, 50%, 75% and exactly at the deadline. Ten hours means a nudge every two and a half hours. Nothing slips.
3. **Real notifications, like a messaging app.** Reminders fire on time with the app closed, with Done and Snooze buttons right on the notification. Birthdays ring every year. Walk near a pharmacy with medicines on your list and your phone taps you on the shoulder.
4. **One calm card at a time.** Aura never floods you. It surfaces the single most important thing, you approve or dismiss it, then the next one comes.
5. **Your day, in one glance.** The Today screen shows your mood check-in, the one card, today's tasks with their deadlines, upcoming birthdays, open promises, and (if you connect it) your calendar.
6. **English and Hindi, properly.** The whole interface, the chat brain, and the voice, both listening and speaking, work in both. Hinglish included.
7. **Privacy that is not a slogan.** Everything Aura knows lives in one file on your phone. No account, no sign-up, no analytics, no tracking. Export your entire memory as a file any day, or delete it. When you subscribe and consent, chat messages are processed by Google Gemini through a relay that stores nothing, and that is disclosed in plain words before the first message. Calendar and contacts are read on the device and never leave it.

## What Aura honestly does not do

iPhone gives no app access to your SMS, WhatsApp, or other apps' notifications, so Aura does not read them and does not pretend to. Email reading is possible only via a Gmail/Outlook sign-in and is on the roadmap, not in the product today. There is no Android version.

## The user journey

1. **Minute one:** download, open, no sign-up. Aura introduces itself in chat with three tappable example lines. You say one thing about your life; Aura catches it and shows you what it caught, as small colored chips.
2. **Day one:** you set a task, Aura asks "till when?", and that evening your phone nudges you at the right moments. The habit forms here: say it once, forget it safely.
3. **Week one:** Today greets you by name, your calendar sits under your mood check-in, a birthday two weeks out appears with a gift idea you mentioned once in passing.
4. **Month one (free, full strength):** you have talked to Aura in Hindi on a walk, it has drafted a birthday note for you, and it has told you a friend has gone quiet. Then it asks, honestly, whether that was worth paying for.
