FIRST-RUN DIARY\n\n- **Minute 0 — open.** No sign-up, no splash, straight in. I land on **Today**: \"good afternoon\", the date, five mood chips, and a violet orb saying \"i'm aura… everything stays on this phone\" (`aura-ux-01-firstrun.png`). Calm, trustworthy, a little empty. *Felt: pleasant, but the marketing said Aura \"introduces itself in chat with three tappable example lines\" at minute one — I'm not in chat, and nothing points me there.*\n- **Minute 1 — Chat.** The promised empty state is here and it's good: \"this is our thread…\", three tappable starters, mic, input (`aura-ux-03`). But directly above the input, before I have said a single word: **\"You're on the simple brain. Try the full one free for a month.\"** *Felt: confused. The marketing said my first month is free \"with everything switched on\". Why am I meeting the dumb version first?* The mic has a little lock on it — voice is paid, also contrary to \"first month free, everything on\".\n- **Minutes 2–4 — the three starters.** \"sam's birthday…\" → \"sam's birthday is jun 28, kept. i'll remember the chocolate cake thing.\" with a **sam · jun 28** chip (`aura-ux-04`). *Felt: smart. This is the product.* \"remind me to call mom tomorrow\" → \"i'll bring 'call mom' back tomorrow\" **and then immediately** \"till when do you want this done?\" (`aura-ux-05`). *Felt: slightly deaf — I just said tomorrow; it should ask \"what time tomorrow?\"* I answered \"by evening\" → \"**locked: tomorrow 7:00 pm. i'll nudge you four times between now and then.**\" (`aura-ux-06`). *Felt: the single best moment of the app. \"locked\" + a concrete plan = trust.* \"i owe jake a coffee by friday\" → \"tracking it, for jake.\" with a **promise** chip (`aura-ux-07`) — but my \"by friday\" disappeared without acknowledgment.\n- **Minutes 5–8 — my real life, in my real language.** \"yaar mujhe kal doodh lena hai\" → *\"i'm listening. tell me about your people…\"* (`aura-ux-08`). \"kal subah mummy ko call karna hai yaad dilana\" → same canned line, word for word (`aura-ux-09`). \"मुझे कल दूध लेना है\" → same line a third time (`aura-ux-10`). *Felt: cheated. The empty state literally says \"in english ya hindi\". Three tries, three identical robot lines. This is the moment I'd close the app on a real evening.*\n- **Minute 9 — plain English also stumbles.** \"pay the electricity bill\" → canned fallback again (`aura-ux-11`). \"i need to pay the electricity bill\" → works, asks \"till when?\", \"in 3 hours\" → \"locked: today 5:07 pm\" (`aura-ux-12`, `aura-ux-13`). *Felt: I've now learned I must speak in magic prefixes (\"remind me to…\", \"i need to…\"). Nobody told me.*\n- **Minute 10 — the ask-then-ignore.** New task \"i need to water the plants\" → Aura asks \"till when?\" → I answer \"**kal subah**\" (the exact phrase the founder's own user-journey doc uses) → canned \"i'm listening…\" fallback (`aura-ux-27`). Retrying in English, \"tomorrow morning\" → \"locked: tomorrow 9:00 am\" (`aura-ux-28`). *Felt: it asked me a question and then didn't understand a completely normal answer, and didn't even say so.*\n- **Minutes 11–13 — the other tabs.** Today now shows the bill under TODAY and the Jake promise under COMING UP as \"aug 21\" (`aura-ux-15`) — first time I learn which Friday it picked. My \"call mom, tomorrow 7pm\" task is **nowhere on Today** — mild panic, did it save? People is genuinely charming: priya \"just met\", jake \"1 open\", sam with a cake emoji (`aura-ux-16`). Notes is an empty second inbox whose pitch (\"files itself into memory… all caught\") sounds identical to Chat (`aura-ux-17`). *Felt: why do I have two places to say things?*\n- **Minute 14 — Settings.** Honest and human (\"That is the deal, in one line\") (`aura-ux-18`–`20`). FRICTION LIST (ranked by real-customer pain, each with the smallest fix)\n\n1. **The offline brain is English-only and the app never admits it.** Empty state says \"english ya hindi\", but `Extraction.swift` is entirely English regex; every Hindi/Hinglish message gets one identical canned line. *Smallest fix, two parts:* (a) **copy** — change the fallback to an honest, varied line: \"didn't catch that one — the simple brain only follows simple english like 'remind me to…'. the full brain speaks hindi properly.\" (b) **a dozen regex lines** in `Extraction.swift` + `DateKit.parseWhen` for the highest-frequency Hinglish: \"yaad dilana / karna hai / lena hai\", \"kal\", \"aaj\", \"subah\", \"shaam\". Not a new feature — the same pattern floor, taught ten more words.\n2. **Aura asks \"till when?\" and then doesn't understand normal answers — silently.** \"kal subah\" → generic fallback; the question is never re-asked (`Store.swift` keeps `pendingDeadlineReminderId` but `send()` falls through to the generic brain). *Smallest fix:* when a deadline is pending and `parseDeadline` fails, reply with a targeted retry — \"didn't catch a time. say 6pm, in 2 hours, or tomorrow morning\" — one branch in `Store.send()`, copy only.\n3. **Minute-one \"simple brain\" banner contradicts \"first month free, everything on\".** The overview promises everything switched on; the app opens pre-gated. *Smallest fix:* reword the banner to lead with the gift: \"your first month of the full brain is free — switch it on\", and show the paywall once right after the user's first \"locked:\" moment (value just demonstrated), not just passively.\n4. **Tomorrow's tasks vanish.** \"call mom — tomorrow 7pm\" appears on no surface except chat scrollback; `TodayView.dueReminders` filters to today, \"Coming up\" only shows birthdays/promises. The customer's trust question — \"did it save?\" — goes unanswered. *Smallest fix:* include reminders due tomorrow in \"Coming up\" with their existing time label; it's a filter change reusing the existing row view.\n5. **Dates are decided silently.** \"by friday\" said on a Friday became Aug 21 and the chat never said so; the promise chip says only \"promise\". *Smallest fix:* `composeLocalReply` echoes the date (\"tracking it, for jake, by aug 21\") and the chip becomes \"promise · aug 21\" — string changes in `Extraction.swift`.\n6. **Hindi UI is half-English where it matters most.** \"today 5:07 अ\" on a task row; every offline reply English. Cause: `DateKit.timeLabel/dayLabel/birthdayLabel` build raw English words and `composeLocalReply` literals are not in the string catalog (the catalog itself is at 100% Hindi — the plumbing exists). *Smallest fix:* wrap ~15 words (\"today\", \"tomorrow\", month/day names) and the ~10 offline reply templates in `String(localized:)`.\n7. **It never asks my name, then can't greet me by name.** The field is buried in Settings. *Smallest fix:* append one line to the first successful chat reply: \"btw, tell me your name in settings and today will greet you properly\" — or a one-line inline field on Today's empty state.\n8. **Paywall has no failure state.** Products fail to load → blank \"Subscription Unavailable\", zero information. *Smallest fix:* a small static fallback view (plans, prices ₹3,000/₹5,000, \"first month free\", support email) shown when the store view has no products.\n9. **\"Draft the note\" can silently do nothing.** `CardView` approves the card then opens `mailto:`; with no mail app configured (common in India) the draft is lost with no feedback. *Smallest fix:* on `openURL` failure, copy the draft to the pasteboard and say so — the ChatView \"Report this response\" flow already does exactly this; reuse the pattern. (Better still, a share sheet — WhatsApp is where these notes will actually go.)\n10. **\"Import a palace file\"** → \"**Import an Aura backup**\". Copy only.\n11. **Notes vs Chat confusion.** *Smallest fix:* one subtitle line in Notes: \"like chat, but quieter — it files things without replying.\"\n12. **Calendar and contacts import are invisible.** Both live behind Settings (\"Show calendar in Today\" toggle, import button). *Smallest fix:* one dismissible ghost row on Today's empty state: \"see your day here — connect calendar in settings.\"\n\n*(Trivial dev nits spotted in passing: stale comment in `PaywallView.swift` line 2 claims voice is Pro-only while `Subscriptions.swift` correctly gives voice to both paid tiers; `Config.swift` still ships placeholder relay/privacy URLs, flagged by its own `#warning`.)*\n\n## 3. USER JOURNEY MAP (as it exists today)\n\n**Download → open (no sign-up — genuinely excellent) → land on empty Today → self-discover Chat → three starters teach the grammar → chips + \"till when?\" + \"locked:\" build trust → permanent \"simple brain\" banner → paywall (Apple sheet, 1-month free trial on both plans) → Gemini consent sheet at first AI message (clear, honest, App-Review-grade, `PaywallView.swift ConsentView`) → month of full AI → ₹3,000/₹5,000 renewal.**\n\nStructural observations: the free floor never expires (good — no hostage-taking), the consent moment is the best-written I've seen in a small app, and the trial requires actively walking through the paywall — the overview's \"month one, then it asks honestly whether it was worth paying for\" moment does not exist in code; nothing marks trial-end except Apple's own machinery.\n\n**The 3–5 cheapest conversion/retention movers:**\n1. **Trigger the paywall on the first \"locked:\" moment** (once), with the banner reworded around the free month as a gift. Converting at the peak-trust second, not at a random banner glance, is the single cheapest conversion change in the app.\n2. **Teach the Hinglish floor the ten phrases above.** The trial habit (\"say it once, forget it safely\") has to survive the user's natural language, or day-2 retention dies before the trial even starts mattering.\n3. **Show tomorrow on Today.** Retention is trust that nothing slips; today the app hides exactly the thing it just promised to hold.\n4. **A day-25 \"was this worth it?\" card** reusing the existing Card engine (type \"trial\", static copy, two buttons) — fulfils the overview's honest-ask promise and catches the renewal decision inside the app instead of in Apple's cold email.\n5. **First-reply name ask** (copy only) so week-one \"greets you by name\" actually happens — cheap warmth, compounding daily.\n\n## 4. WHAT FEELS MISSING (marketing promise vs felt reality)\n\n- **\"Everything switched on\" for the first month** — never felt; I met the locked mic and \"simple brain\" banner in my first sixty seconds.\n- **\"English and Hindi, properly. Hinglish included.\"** — the paid brain would deliver (the `Brain.swift` prompts genuinely mirror language, and its extractor knows \"yaad dilana\"); the floor I could actually touch speaks no Hindi and won't admit it.\n- **\"Today greets you by name\"** — it never asked my name.\n- **Calendar under my mood check-in** — exists, but behind a Settings toggle I had to hunt for.\n- **Voice both ways** — locked; judged from `Speech.swift`/`ChatView.swift` (mic → paywall; spoken replies toggle for paid; en-IN/hi-IN voices selectable). Plumbing looks real.\n- **Place nudges** (\"walk near a pharmacy…\") — real and thoughtfully Indian (`LocationKit.swift` matches \"dawai\", \"kirana\", \"sabzi\", \"chai\"), but nothing anywhere teaches the magic phrase \"remind me to X **when i'm at** Y\", so most users will never fire one in their life.\n- **What I'd miss most daily as the absent small thing:** WhatsApp-ready sharing of the birthday/check-in drafts. Aura writes lovely little notes (`CardEngine.swift` drafts) and then tries to send them through email, which is not where any of my people are. A share sheet is the missing two square centimeters between \"cute\" and \"used weekly\".\n\n## 5. WOULD YOU PAY?\n\n**Honest verdict: not yet at ₹3,000/year — but I'm close, and I'm the target customer.** The skeleton is worth paying for: the four-nudge engine with sleep-shifting (`DateKit.quarterPoints`), truthful badges, Done/Snooze on the notification (`Notifications.swift`), the one-calm-card discipline, no account, my data in one exportable file. That is a real product with real taste, and ₹250/month is fair for \"nothing slips\".\n\nBut the month that is supposed to convince me would today be spent code-switching for an assistant whose free floor goes mute in my own language, silently reschedules my Fridays, and hides tomorrow. Those are trust papercuts, and a memory product lives entirely on trust.\n\n**The single change that most moves my answer:** make the everyday floor honest and bilingual — the ten Hinglish patterns plus the \"didn't catch that\" fallback (Friction #1/#2). The day \"yaar kal doodh lena hai\" comes back as \"kal yaad dila dungi 🥛 — till when?\", this app feels like the friend the marketing sold me, the habit forms in my language, and ₹3,000 stops being a question.
