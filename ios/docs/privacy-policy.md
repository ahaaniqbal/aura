# aura privacy policy

last updated: 14 august 2026

aura is an assistant that remembers people, birthdays, promises and reminders for you. this page explains what happens to your data, in plain words.

## the short version

everything aura knows about you lives in one file on your phone. we do not have accounts, we do not run analytics, we do not track you, and we cannot see your memory. the only time anything leaves your phone is when you turn on AI chat, and even then nothing is stored on any server.

## your memory stays on your phone

all your personal data (people, notes, birthdays, reminders, chat history) is kept in a single file inside the app on your device. there is no account to create and no copy on our servers, because we do not have servers that store user data.

## AI chat, only if you choose it

AI chat is optional. it is off until you subscribe and clearly agree to turn it on.

when it is on, the message you type and the relevant parts of your memory are sent to Google Gemini, an AI service by Google, so it can write a reply. the request passes through a small relay we run. the relay is stateless: it forwards your message and forgets it. we store nothing, we log nothing about the content, and we cannot read your conversations later.

if you never subscribe, nothing you type is ever sent anywhere.

## voice

if you use voice, your speech is turned into text on your phone when your language supports it. when on-device recognition is not available for your language, apple's speech service processes the audio under apple's privacy terms. we never receive your recordings.

## notifications

reminders and birthday alerts are local notifications scheduled by your phone. they do not go through any server.

## location

if you turn on place reminders, your location is processed on your phone to notice when you are near a saved place. your location is never sent to us.

## no tracking, no analytics, no ads

we do not use analytics tools, advertising ids, or trackers of any kind. we do not sell or share your data, because we never have it.

## export and delete

you can export your entire memory file, or delete everything, at any time from inside the app. deleting the app also deletes your data, since it only exists on your device.

## payments

subscriptions are handled by apple through the App Store. we never see your payment details.

## changes

if this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
